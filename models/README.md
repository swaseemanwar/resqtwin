# Models

Store reviewable MATLAB, Simulink, and Stateflow source models here.

For each model, document:

- purpose and boundary;
- required MATLAB release, products, and support packages;
- inputs, outputs, units, sample times, and assumptions;
- initialization and reproduction steps; and
- validation cases and acceptance criteria.

Source formats such as `.slx` may be committed when needed. Caches, autosaves, compiled models, and generated-code directories must remain untracked.
