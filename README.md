# ResQTwin

ResQTwin is our team repository for **MATRIX 2026**, under the **AI in Disaster Management** track, Problem Statement 7: **“Disaster Area Digital Twin.”**

We are currently in the **problem-framing** phase. The final idea, system design, and hardware setup have not been chosen.

## What is confirmed

- Event: MATRIX 2026
- Track: AI in Disaster Management
- Problem: PS7, “Disaster Area Digital Twin”
- Primary event tools: MATLAB and Simulink

## What we still need to decide

- the disaster or hazard to focus on;
- the main user and the decision we want to support;
- the area and scope of the demonstration;
- the data, sensors, and hardware we can use; and
- the simplest idea we can build and test well.

Ideas being discussed are **PROPOSED**. A team decision is final only when it is marked **LOCKED**. A replaced decision is marked **SUPERSEDED**.

## What we are doing now

1. Collect the complete official PS7 wording and organizer clarifications.
2. Share and compare possible use cases.
3. List the MATLAB licences, hardware, sensors, and skills available to the team.
4. Agree on one small, measurable demonstration before building the full system.

## Working in this repository

Keep changes small, clear, and easy to review. MATLAB and Simulink are the primary tools for now; other tools can be added when the project needs them.

Before making changes, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Shared requirements, research, and decisions belong under [docs/](docs/).
